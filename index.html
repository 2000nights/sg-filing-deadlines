How each deadline is worked out

These are the rules the calculator uses for a non-listed private company. All of them were checked against the official pages on 30 Oct 2026.

Annual return (ACRA)

7 months after FYE (8 with a branch register outside Singapore)

Filed on BizFile. A solvent exempt private company does not attach financial statements, but declares solvency. A dormant relevant company does not need financial statements either. The fee is S$60, and the late penalty is S$300, or S$600 if more than 3 months late.

Sources: ACRA annual return deadlines, ACRA late filing penalties

AGM and financial statements

AGM within 6 months; statements to members within 5 months if there is no AGM

If you hold an AGM, the financial statements go to members at least 14 days before it, so the latest date is 6 months minus 14 days. A private company that sends its statements within 5 months does not need an AGM, and the 5-month limit also applies when members have dispensed with AGMs. A dormant relevant company needs neither.

Sources: ACRA AGM due dates, Companies Act s175 and s175A, s203

Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI)

3 months after FYE

Not needed when revenue is S$5 million or less and ECI is nil. Both conditions must hold. Filing earlier gives more GIRO instalments for the tax.

Source: IRAS ECI filing

Form C-S, C-S (Lite), Form C

30 Nov of the Year of Assessment (15 Dec via #SFFS)

Income for a financial year is taxed in the next Year of Assessment, so a year ending 31 Mar 2026 is YA 2027 and due on 30 Nov 2027. Form C-S filed through IRAS-listed accounting software gets until 15 Dec for YA 2023 to YA 2027. A dormant company files the Form for Dormant Company unless IRAS has waived the return.

Sources: IRAS filing season 2026, IRAS #SFFS

GST F5

1 month after each accounting period ends

Most businesses file quarterly; some file monthly. File a nil return even with no sales. The late filing penalty is S$200, then S$200 for each completed month, up to S$10,000 per return.

Sources: IRAS GST due dates, IRAS late GST filing

CPF and IR8A (if you have employees)

CPF by month end (14th of next month before interest); IR8A by 1 Mar

CPF contributions are due on the last day of the month the wages are for. Interest of 1.5% a month applies if they are still unpaid on the 14th of the next month. IR8A for the previous calendar year goes to staff, or to IRAS under the Auto-Inclusion Scheme (compulsory with 5 or more employees), by 1 March.

Sources: CPF Board contributions, IRAS IR8A and AIS

Frequently asked questions

When is the annual return due for a Singapore company?

A non-listed company files its annual return with ACRA within 7 months after its financial year end, or within 8 months if it keeps a branch register of members outside Singapore. For a 31 December year end, that is 31 July of the next year. The filing fee is S$60, and the late lodgment penalty is S$300, or S$600 if the return is more than 3 months late.

Does my private company have to hold an AGM?

A non-listed company must hold its AGM within 6 months after its financial year end. A private company does not need to hold one if it sends its financial statements to members within 5 months after the year end, or if its members have passed a resolution to dispense with AGMs (the statements must still go out within 5 months). A dormant relevant company does not need an AGM. A member can still ask for an AGM up to 14 days before the 6-month deadline.

When is Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI) due, and when can I skip it?

ECI is due within 3 months after the financial year end. A company does not need to file it if its revenue for the year is S$5 million or less and its ECI is nil. Filing within 1 or 2 months of the year end gives more GIRO instalments.

When is Form C-S or Form C due?

The corporate income tax return for a financial year is filed in the following Year of Assessment (YA), by 30 November of that YA. For YA 2023 to YA 2027, a Form C-S filed through IRAS-listed accounting software (#SFFS) is due by 15 December instead.

When is the GST F5 return due?

A GST-registered business files its GST F5 return and pays any GST one month after the end of each accounting period, which is usually quarterly. For a quarter ending 31 March, it is due by 30 April. A nil return is still required when there was no activity.

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server, and the page uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.
